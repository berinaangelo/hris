Team Reviews — three recommendations

The manager-only workspace under Team → Team Reviews (decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md) — where a manager does the actual work described in decisions/performance-review-kpi-based-not-form-builder.md: opens a cycle, sets a few KPIs, and fills in actual results, scores, and a comment per KPI once the cycle ends. This is the manager-authored counterpart to the employee's read-only decisions/ux-pages/my-reviews.html. What changes across the three is how a manager moves between direct reports to get that work done: a flat roster with row actions and a shared modal, an always-visible split with an editable detail panel, or a status-first card grid.

Ahead of the backlog, on purpose: performance reviews/goals is still post-MVP backlog, not committed to a version — this mockup exists because the nav slot is already reserved. Same 5-person team as decisions/ux-pages/team-approvals.html and decisions/ux-pages/team-calendar.html (manager Ramon Dela Cruz). Cycle content follows the fixed KPI structure on file (kpi_name / target / actual / score 1–5 / comment); an "On PIP" badge is Caution per decisions/ui/badge-system-four-categories.md, and its outcome field (Passed/Not Passed/Extended) is recorded at close — not shown active here since no PIP closes in this persona set. The Team tab's live count badge aggregates pending approvals and pending reviews together, per decisions/ui/notifications-nav-badge-counts.md; it's kept at the same illustrative 4 used in the other Team-tab mockups rather than recomputed for this page. All editable fields shown here are static, illustrative content — this is a comparison mockup, not a wired form.
