Team Calendar — three recommendations

The manager-only view under Team → Team Calendar (decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md). Directly serves the plan's own god moment: "who's out this week is answerable by looking, not asking." What changes across the three is the shape of the "look" — a spatial month grid, a comparative row-by-row timeline, or a plain day-by-day agenda for the current week.

Carried over, not re-decided here: this is a read-only view over existing time-off request data (see decisions/ui/time-off-list-plus-modal.md) — no new data model, no separate scheduling feature. Only Approved and Pending requests appear (Pending shown as tentative, tinted per decisions/ui/badge-system-four-categories.md); a Rejected request means the employee isn't actually out, so it never appears here. Shift schedules and attendance are out of scope — that's the separate, still-backlog Time & Attendance feature. Scoped to the viewing manager's own direct reports, same team as decisions/ui/team-approvals-inbox-inline-actions.md.

Why this one

The whole month is visible at once — every absence for the period is on screen without paging or scrolling, and the spatial layout matches how most people already read a calendar.

Best when

The question is date-first ("what's the week of the 24th look like"). Weaker at the question "is anyone else out at the same time as X" — figuring out overlap means scanning several cells and holding names in your head, which Option 2's row layout answers directly.
