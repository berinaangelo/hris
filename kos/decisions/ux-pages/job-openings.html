Job Openings — three recommendations

The HR-Admin landing page for Recruitment (Company → Recruitment, decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md), first screen of projects/hris/features/recruitment-ats/PLAN.md's core flow — "HR creates a job opening." One row/card per opening: title, open/closed status, and how far along its candidates are in the fixed New → Interviewing → Offer → Hired/Rejected pipeline. Clicking into an opening goes to Job Opening Detail + Candidate Pipeline, a separate not-yet-built page — nothing here edits a candidate's stage directly. Unlike Payroll Runs, more than one opening can be open at the same time, so there's no single "the one active run" to pin.

Deliberately deferred, built anyway for comparison: per kos/decisions/ats-checker-reuse-parked-for-recruitment.md and the user's own roadmap, Recruitment stays last in the build order until the public candidate-facing application form exists — this mockup covers only the HR-Admin list. Viewing persona: Andrea Cruz, HR Admin, today is Aug 22, 2026. The link is an application-form URL, not a public job-ad page — HR posts the actual ad on JobStreet/Indeed/LinkedIn and points its Apply button here; shown as illustrative (hris.app/apply/…), no real subdomain wired. Status badges follow decisions/ui/badge-system-four-categories.md: Open (actively hiring) reads Positive since it's the wanted working state, Closed reads Neutral — no judgment on a filled-vs-cancelled opening, that detail lives on the Detail page. Pipeline counts are illustrative placeholders, not computed. Option 02's card grid also carries a card/list view toggle, card selected by default — reusing decisions/ui/people-directory-card-grid-with-list-toggle.md's exact mechanic rather than inventing a new one.

Why this one

Every opening, open or closed, on one sortable/filterable surface — matches decisions/ui/rails-datatable-pagy-turbo-frame-pattern.md's default table shape and is the fastest to build. Good when the list is mostly flat browsing.

Trade-off

Open and closed postings compete for the same rows at the same visual weight — the thing HR actually opens this page to check ("what am I hiring for right now") isn't separated from postings nobody needs to look at again.

Why this one

Reuses the card-grid language already shipped on People Directory and Rate Tables — each opening reads as a self-contained unit (title, status, pipeline shape, one-click actions), and closed cards are visually muted rather than removed, so nothing disappears from view. Card stays the default per decisions/ui/people-directory-card-grid-with-list-toggle.md's own reasoning — friendly for a small, growing list — but the same view toggle puts a denser, sortable table one click away for whoever wants to scan every posting at once.

Trade-off

Two view states to keep visually consistent instead of one — the list view has to carry the same title/status/pipeline/link columns the cards show, or switching views would feel like a different page. Chosen anyway: the same trade was already accepted once for People Directory, and it's cheap to reuse the identical mechanic here.

Why this one

Separates "what am I actively hiring for" from archive the same way decisions/ui/compliance-certifications-pinned-attention-full-list.md separates needs-attention from the full sorted list — open postings get full-card weight up top with pipeline counts visible without a click; closed postings collapse behind one disclosure toggle, closed by default, so the page opens on exactly what needs a decision today.

Trade-off

An extra click to see anything closed — fine if HR genuinely never revisits filled roles, but if there's a habit of double-checking past postings (comp benchmarking, reposting a similar role), that history is one step further away than Options 1–2.
