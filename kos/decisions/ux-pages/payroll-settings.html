13th Month Pay — three recommendations

Not a standalone page — a company-level toggle (thirteenth_month_pay_enabled, default true) that lives on the Payroll Settings screen, per decisions/thirteenth-month-pay-mandatory-in-ph.md. 13th month pay is legally mandated for rank-and-file private-sector employees in the Philippines (PD 851) with only narrow exemptions — managerial staff, or an employer already paying an equivalent benefit under another name. All three options sit on the same Payroll Settings shell; what changes is how much weight the toggle itself carries.

Pay Schedule and Rate Tables sections are shown for page context only — identical across all three options, not the subject of this comparison (Rate Tables already has its own decision, see decisions/ui/rate-tables-landing-cards-edit-drawer.md). Overtime Rules and Deduction Defaults (added 2026-08-23) are locked placeholders, not part of any comparison — disabled fields, no interactive state, parked here in case payroll scope expands to cover them later; per the plan, OT and deductions stay manual line items per run for v2. Projected payout figures are illustrative mock data, not computed. Viewing persona: Andrea Cruz, HR Admin, today is Aug 22, 2026 — the toggle starts ON (the legally-expected default) in every option; click it to preview the OFF state.

Why this one

Zero extra weight — a settings toggle looks and behaves exactly like every other switch on this screen. Fastest to build, fastest to scan, matches the "it's just a company preference" mental model most settings rows carry.

Trade-off

Nothing distinguishes this from an ordinary preference toggle — no signal that flipping it off is a legal decision with narrow exemptions, not a style choice. An HR admin exploring settings could switch it off by accident with no friction at all.

Turn on 13th month pay?

Why this one

Treats the toggle as the compliance decision it actually is — clicking it open a confirmation naming PD 851 and requiring an exemption reason before it takes effect, the same weight decisions/rails-db-transactions-locking-idempotency.md gives to payroll finalization's own irreversible steps. A "Compliant · PD 851" badge is the default steady state; a caution badge and the recorded reason only show up on the deviation, matching decisions/ui/badge-system-four-categories.md's own rule.

Trade-off

One more click and a required field to turn it off, versus Options 1–2's instant toggle — the right amount of friction only if disabling this really is rare and consequential enough to warrant a recorded reason, not routine housekeeping.
