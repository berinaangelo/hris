Login page — three recommendations

Same tokens throughout — Ink & Amber, Archivo/Work Sans, Lucide-style icons, inline-only validation. What changes across the three is how much visual weight the screen spends before the employee gets to their actual balance or inbox.

Not yet covered by a decision on file: auth flow specifics. All three follow the provisioning model already set in the v1 plan — HR adds the employee, so there's no sign-up link, only a pointer to contact HR. "Remember me" and "Forgot password" are conventional additions on top of that, not decided anywhere yet — cheap to drop if unwanted.
