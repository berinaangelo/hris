The "Hired" handoff — three recommendations

What happens the instant HR clicks "Mark Hired" on an Offer-stage candidate in Job Opening Detail — the plan's own payoff moment: "marking someone 'Hired' creates their employee record," candidate data becoming employee profile data with no retyping. This step was explicitly left unscoped when the kanban pipeline layout was decided ("assumed to be a lightweight confirm dialog, not modeled") — these three options model it for real.

Not yet a decision on file: the application form only captures name, email, phone, resume, and a note — none of the org facts (department, manager, employment type) Add Employee's own record needs. All three options below assume job title defaults from the opening itself (here, "Senior Backend Engineer") since that's already known; department/manager/start date/type still need HR input somewhere in the handoff. Candidate: Ramil Espino, Offer stage, offer accepted.

Why this one

Fastest — one click plus a start date confirms the whole handoff, no navigation away from the pipeline. Matches the plan's own throwaway framing ("a lightweight confirm dialog") and needs no new page or drawer pattern.

Trade-offs

Department, manager, and employment type — the org facts the application form never captured — are left blank at creation, so every Hired candidate needs a follow-up trip to Employee Detail before they're routing approvals correctly. Cheapest to build, but defers real setup work rather than finishing it in one motion.
