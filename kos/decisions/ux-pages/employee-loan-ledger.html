Loan Ledger — three recommendations

The content of the "Loan Ledger" tab reserved on decisions/ux-pages/employee-detail.html — per-employee, reached the same way Benefits is: a top-level tab next to Profile, not a standalone page. What changes across the three is how one-vs- several concurrent loans are shown and how much the payoff progress is surfaced: a single master table with a row-edit drawer, repeatable progress cards, or a compact expandable list with paid-off loans tucked out of the way.

Carried over, not re-decided here: per decisions/cash-advance-vs-loan-ledger-distinction.md, this covers loans only (SSS Salary Loan, Pag-IBIG MPL/Calamity Loan, company loans) — cash advances stay a manual payroll deduction line, not shown here. Fields in scope: loan type, total amount, monthly amortization (typed in from the loan statement, never computed), and remaining installments. A loan auto-adds as a deduction each payroll run, counts down, and marks itself paid off at zero — no interest computation, application/approval workflow, or early payoff/refinancing. Status badges reuse decisions/ui/badge-system-four-categories.md: Active → Neutral, Paid off → Positive. The "X% paid" progress shown in every option is computed on the fly from the three stored fields (total − remaining × monthly), not a new persisted field. An employee can carry more than one loan at once (e.g. an SSS Salary Loan alongside a company loan) — same "don't assume exactly one" lesson as Benefits, so every option handles zero-to-many rows. Option 1 (chosen) is the flat table + edit drawer: this data is ledger-shaped (running balances, payoff counts) more than profile-shaped, and it's the fourth reuse of the same drawer mechanic already established on Payroll Run Detail, Time & Attendance, and Rate Tables.

Why this one

Ledger data reads best as a table — total, monthly amortization, remaining count, and payoff progress line up in columns for direct comparison across loans, and every row stays visible without a click. Best when
