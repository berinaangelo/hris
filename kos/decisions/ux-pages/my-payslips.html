My Payslips — three recommendations

The employee's own payslip history, under Me → My Payslips (decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md). Read-only — payroll generates these, the employee only views/downloads. What changes across the three is how someone gets from "24 cutoffs a year" down to one they actually want to open: a year-filtered table with a modal, a pinned latest payslip with a swappable table below, or a statement-style feed grouped by year.

Ahead of the roadmap, on purpose: payroll is v2 — committed, but deferred until after v1 ships — yet decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md already reserves the "My Payslips" nav slot, so this mockup exists now the same way My Reviews did. Figures shown (₱22,500 basic pay, SSS/ PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG/BIR amounts) are illustrative placeholders, not real rate-table output — the actual amounts come from editable statutory tables per decisions/statutory-deductions-as-editable-data-not-code.md. The loan line reflects decisions/cash-advance-vs-loan-ledger-distinction.md (a typed-in monthly amortization, not computed); the December 13th Month Pay entry reflects decisions/thirteenth-month-pay-mandatory-in-ph.md.
