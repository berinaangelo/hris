Job Opening Detail + Candidate Pipeline — three recommendations

Where "View pipeline" from Job Openings lands — one opening's full detail plus every candidate against the plan's fixed New → Interviewing → Offer → Hired/Rejected stages (projects/hris/features/recruitment-ats/PLAN.md). Stage changes are a dropdown per candidate, not drag-and-drop, per the plan's own wording. Marking someone Hired is the plan's payoff moment — it auto-creates their employee record, so every option surfaces that outcome distinctly rather than treating Hired as just another badge.

Same opening, same ten candidates, identical tokens across all three options: Senior Backend Engineer — 3 New, 3 Interviewing, 2 Offer, 1 Hired, 1 Rejected. Viewing persona: Andrea Cruz, HR Admin, today is Aug 22, 2026. The opening's link is an application-form URL, not a public job-ad page — HR posts the actual ad on JobStreet/Indeed/LinkedIn and points its Apply button here. Résumé links and the application link are illustrative, no file storage or real subdomain wired. Per-candidate notes shown are the free-text field from decisions/ats-checker-reuse-parked-for-recruitment.md's parent plan — interview feedback, not a structured scorecard. Marking a candidate Hired opening a confirmation step before creating the employee record is out of scope for this layout comparison — assume a lightweight confirm, not modeled here. Stage badge colors follow decisions/ui/badge-system-four-categories.md's own Recruitment mapping: New → Neutral, Interviewing/Offer → Caution, Hired → Positive, Rejected → Negative.

Why this one

Reuses Team Reviews' exact roster-list + detail-panel mechanic — one line per candidate on the left (name, applied recency, stage badge), full contact info, résumé, applicant note, editable HR notes, and the stage control together on the right, no separate page or modal. Trade-off

Only one candidate is fully visible at a time — comparing two candidates side by side (both Offer-stage, say) means clicking back and forth, unlike Options 1–2 where every card or row is on screen together.
