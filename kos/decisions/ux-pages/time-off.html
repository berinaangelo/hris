Time Off — three recommendations

Request list + new request form, under Me → Time Off (decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md). What changes across the three is where the "new request" form lives relative to the history list — a modal over it, a permanent panel beside it, or a full-width view toggled with it. Content and fields are identical in all three.

Carried over, not re-decided here: approval is single-level only — the request goes straight to the employee's manager, no configurable chain (see decisions/approval-chains-scrapped-fallback-design.md). Balance figures (12.5 of 15 days) are placeholder content, same as the Home mockup. Leave types (Vacation, Sick, Emergency, Others) and whether the "Reason" field is required are not yet a policy decision — shown here as optional in all three.
