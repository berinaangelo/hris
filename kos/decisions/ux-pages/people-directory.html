People Directory — three recommendations

The HR-Admin-only company roster under Company → People Directory (decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md) — the entry point into every employee's record, and where "Add Employee" starts. What changes across the three is how an HR admin browses/finds someone: a dense searchable table, sections grouped by department, or a photo-forward card grid.

Carried over, not re-decided here: Company tab is HR-Admin-only, absent (not filtered) for anyone else — this persona (Andrea Cruz) has no direct reports, so her nav shows Me/Company only, no Team tab, demonstrating that rule. Active employees show no status badge — only "Offboarded" gets one (Neutral), per decisions/ui/badge-system-four-categories.md's own "badges are for deviation, not every state" rule; toggle below previews it. Row density matches decisions/ui/data-tables-comfortable-density.md (~46px, zebra-striped). Search/filter controls are illustrative here — a real build follows the sanitized-params pagination guard in decisions/rails-pagination-and-batch-export-processing.md. Clicking a row/card goes to Employee Detail, not built yet — shown as a hover affordance only. Same Engineering team as decisions/ux-pages/team-approvals.html (Ramon Dela Cruz's 5 direct reports), plus a few more departments added to show this at company-wide scale rather than one team repeated. Option 3 (chosen) adds a card/list view toggle in the toolbar — card is the v1 default, list reuses Option 1's table exactly, switchable any time.

Why this one

The friendliest way to browse a company you're getting to know — good for a new HR admin, or anyone trying to put a face to a name. Department and tenure both visible per card. The card/list toggle in the toolbar means the scale concern below is never a dead end — switching to list is one click, reusing Option 1's table exactly, no separate page or lost context.

Best when

Browsing rather than searching, at a headcount where a grid stays scannable — which is why card is the v1 default. Takes more vertical space per person than a table, but the toggle means switching to list for a denser scan or a big search session costs nothing.
