Team Approvals — three recommendations

The manager-only inbox under Team → Approvals (decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md) — the "Approve" step of the v1 primary flow. The plan's own god moment is the design brief here: "manager approves from one inbox with balance shown inline — no cross-checking a spreadsheet." What changes across the three is how much friction sits between seeing a request and deciding on it.

Carried over, not re-decided here: approval is single-level only, straight to the requester's manager — see decisions/approval-chains-scrapped-fallback-design.md. Whether a rejection requires a note is not yet a policy decision; Option 2 shows an optional one, Options 1 and 3 keep reject a single click, consistent with the fallback design's own framing ("same approve/reject mechanism, just run twice — no new state machinery"). This page is Team-tab only — absent entirely for anyone without direct reports, per the absent-not-filtered rule in the navigation decision.

Why this one

The most literal build of "one inbox, balance inline, approve/deny in one click" — every fact needed to decide is already on the row, including a caution note when approving would leave someone with very little balance left.

Best when

A manager processes several requests in one sitting and wants speed over ceremony. Riskier for a reflexive click — nothing stands between seeing a row and approving it, so this relies entirely on the row itself carrying enough context.

Adds a deliberate read-before-you-act step — a manager sees the reason and a plain-language balance warning before Approve/Reject even appear, reducing the odds of a rubber-stamp approval on a request that deserves a second look.
