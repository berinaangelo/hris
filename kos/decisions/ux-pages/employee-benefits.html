Employee Benefits — three recommendations

The content of the "Benefits" tab reserved on decisions/ux-pages/employee-detail.html — per-employee, record-keeping only (plan name, provider, effectivity date, dependents), reached the same way Loan Ledger will be: a top-level tab next to Profile, not a standalone page. What changes across the three is how one-vs-several benefit enrollments are modeled: one flat section, repeatable per-plan cards, or a compact expandable list.

Carried over, not re-decided here: record-keeping only — no enrollment workflow, eligibility rules, cost-sharing, open enrollment, claims, or carrier integrations, per projects/hris/features/benefits/PLAN.md and decisions/vendor-fragmented-features-record-keeping-only.md. HR types the plan in after enrolling the employee directly with the carrier. Fields in scope: plan name, provider, effectivity date, and a simple dependents list (name + relationship). No status badge — there's no deviation state to flag here, unlike Loan Ledger's Active/Paid-off, per decisions/ui/badge-system-four-categories.md's own rule. Every option demos both a populated state and the decisions/ui/empty-states-guided.md empty state for a brand-new hire with nothing on record yet. Option 2 (chosen) is repeatable plan cards: one card per enrollment, each independently pencil-editable, "+ Add plan" to append more — the only option that doesn't assume exactly one benefit plan per employee, matching the common PH SME pattern of HMO plus a separately-provisioned life or accident policy.
