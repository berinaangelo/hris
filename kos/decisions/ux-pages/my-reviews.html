My Reviews — three recommendations

The employee's own finished review cycles, under Me → My Reviews (decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md). Read-only for the employee throughout — a manager fills in KPIs and scores, never the employee. What changes across the three is how someone moves between review cycles: a list with a detail modal, a always-visible master-detail split, or an expandable timeline.

Ahead of the backlog, on purpose: performance reviews/goals is scoped post-MVP backlog, not committed to a version — this mockup exists because decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md already reserves the nav slot, not because the feature is scheduled. Content follows the fixed KPI structure already on file (decisions/performance-review-kpi-based-not-form-builder.md): kpi_name / target / actual / score(1–5) / comment, overall rating as a simple average. A published PIP cycle's outcome (Passed/Not Passed/Extended, via BadgePresenter) shows as a badge next to its rating — demonstrated on the H2 2024 cycle below (Option 2). A cycle in progress shows its KPI targets (no scores yet) beneath the "your manager is completing this cycle" note — synced 2026-08-24 to match the real review_cycles/_detail.html.erb, which renders that preview even before scoring starts.
