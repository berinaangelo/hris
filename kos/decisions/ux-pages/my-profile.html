My Profile — three recommendations

View/edit for v1, under Me → My Profile (decisions/ui/navigation-me-team-company.md). What changes across the three is how editing is scoped and surfaced — one whole-page toggle, per-section edit, or a summary page with edit pulled into a modal. Content and fields are identical in all three.

Field split, not yet a decision on file: employment facts (job title, department, manager, start date) stay HR-managed and read-only everywhere, matching the plan's "HR adds a hire once and it's correct everywhere — never edited twice." Contact and emergency-contact fields are the only ones an employee edits directly. Fixed schema throughout, per the v1 plan's "not a custom-field builder" — no per-company or per-field configuration in any of the three.
